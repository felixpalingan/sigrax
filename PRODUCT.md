## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, Modern Vanilla CSS3 Design System, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.

## Users

- **Plant Directors & Factory Operations Heads**: Need executive oversight, maintenance ROI metrics (MTTR, MTBF), downtime tracking, and overall operational reliability.
- **Maintenance Supervisors & Chief Engineers**: Need automated preventive scheduling, work order dispatching, technician workload balancing, and ISO 55000 / TPM compliance tracking.
- **Field Technicians & Mechanics**: Need quick mobile-friendly work order access, breakdown logging, spare parts checkouts, and inspection checklists on the factory floor.

## Product Purpose

Sigrax CMMS (*Computerized Maintenance Management System*) is an Indonesian-built enterprise maintenance management platform designed to automate, schedule, and monitor industrial manufacturing plant assets, preventive maintenance workflows, spare parts inventories, and repair work orders in one unified system.

## Positioning

- **Zero Annual License Fee (Model Tanpa Biaya Lisensi Tahunan)**: Unlike overseas enterprise CMMS software with recurring per-user yearly subscription lock-ins, Sigrax offers a transparent, cost-effective ownership model.
- **Local 24/7 On-Site & Digital Engineering Support**: Dedicated Indonesian technical consultants and support engineers offering hands-on SOP customization, Excel data migration, on-site go-live accompaniment, and factory floor technician training.
- **11 Integrated Industrial Modules**: Comprehensive end-to-end operational coverage from asset master data and preventive maintenance to warehouse spare parts tracking and executive analytics.

## Operating Context

- Indonesian manufacturing facilities, factories, food & beverage processing plants, automotive suppliers, heavy industry, textile, chemical, and industrial manufacturing plants across Indonesia.
- Multi-tier team interaction: from rugged factory floors (operator/technician tickets) to executive boardroom presentations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **11 Core Modules**:
  1. Executive Dashboard & Metrics (MTTR, MTBF, Real-Time Downtime tracking)
  2. Master Data Hub (Sites, Locations, Techs, Vendors, Units)
  3. Asset & Machine Management (Digital specifications, breakdown history, manuals)
  4. Preventive Maintenance (Automated calendar schedules, recurring tasks, inspection logs)
  5. Work Order & Ticket Management (Real-time breakdown alerts, task assignments)
  6. Task Checklists & Completed Work Records (Photo verification, digital sign-off)
  7. Spare Parts & Warehouse Inventory (Min-max stock alerts, part usage logs)
  8. Employee & Technician Registry (Skill levels, assignments, shift tracking)
  9. Equipment History & Lifecycle Audits (MTBF/MTTR tracking, ISO audit readiness)
  10. Cost & Budget Analytics (Maintenance vs budget variance)
  11. Multi-Plant / Multi-Site Support (Centralized monitoring across multiple factory locations)
- **Technical Constraints**: No heavyweight frontend framework build pipeline needed; uses standard web standards (HTML5/CSS/JS) for maximum compatibility, instant loading, and seamless hosting.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity Over Complexity**: Industrial maintenance managers are busy; every interface must be immediately scannable with high contrast and zero clutter.
2. **Industrial Durability & Precision**: Modern, enterprise-grade feel reflecting precision machinery, reliable uptime, and rigorous ISO/TPM standards.
3. **Action-Oriented Workflows**: Direct conversion paths for factory managers to schedule live product demos and consult with local engineers.
